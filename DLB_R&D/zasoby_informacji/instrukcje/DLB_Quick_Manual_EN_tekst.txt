1. Scope & Safety

   · Install and set up only by qualified electricians.
   · Isolate power before wiring; lock-out/tag-out where applicable.
   · Current Transformer (CT) orientation must be correct; never energize

        with CT secondary open-circuited.
   · Set the Configured Supply Limit (CSL) according to incoming cable size,

        breaker rating, and local codes.
   · Install inside a dry, dust-free, ventilated distribution board; this device is

        not weatherproof.
   · Keep the wireless antenna away from strong EMI sources; ensure proper

        strain relief on cables.

2. Description & Operating Principle

   The DLB monitors the household's total current (single/three-phase) and
   allocates optimal charging current to 1-3 wall boxes in real time, so that the
   site limit is never exceeded.

   · Dynamic Load Balancing: per-EV optimization to use every available amp
        without exceeding the limit.

   · Safety Buffer: default usable ceiling is 90% of the Configured Supply Limit
        (10% buffer).

   · Communications: 433 MHz wireless (SMA antenna) or RS485; target
        current is delivered to wall box in real time.

   · Fairness & Constraints: each wall box is capped by its own requested
        current; any branch < minimum sustaining current is paused.

3. Parts & I/O (Refer to Photo)

   · Antenna (Gold-plated SMA): 433 MHz wireless.
   · Power input: AC 100-240 V, 50/60 Hz, L / N / PE.
   · CT inputs: L1 / L2 / L3 Current Transformers.
   · Pairing button: Hold 5 s to enter pairing mode (clears all existing links);

        press once to finish early (otherwise times out after ~2 min).
   · Link LEDs: link status for up to three wall boxes.
   · 8-way DIP switch: sets Configured Supply Limit (1-255 A).

4. Installation

   · Mount inside the main distribution board for short CT runs and
        convenient power/RS485 wiring.

   · CTs: Clamp on the incoming L1/L2/L3, upstream of all branch breakers
        (not on branches). Arrow toward load; on three-phase, monitor all phases
        and limit by the heaviest phase.

   · 433 MHz antenna (default): keep vertical, ideally outside metal
        enclosures. Where site conditions--e.g., walls or large metal structures--
        impede wireless connectivity, select RS485.

   · RS485: use either 433 MHz or RS485 -- not both. Switching to RS485
        requires opening the wall box and moving the internal selector to
        "RS485" before installation. Wire A/B correctly.

5. Settings

   5.1 Configured Supply Limit (DIP 1-255 A)

      Use the 8-way DIP (weights 128/64/32/16/8/4/2/1 A). Toggle ON to sum to
      the desired amperage. The DLB applies 90% of this value as the usable
      ceiling (10% safety buffer).

      Examples: 75 A = 64+8+2+1; 62 A = 32+16+8+4+2; 50 A = 32+16+2.

   5.2 Minimum Sustaining Current

      Recommended 6 A. If a branch would receive <6 A, that wall box is paused
      until headroom returns.

6. Pairing & Operation

   6.1 Pairing

      · Warning: entering pairing mode clears all existing links; please re-pair
           ALL wall boxes (1-3) simultaneously.

      · DLB: hold the Pairing Button for 5 s until all three link LEDs flash; the
           pairing mode times out after 2 min.

      · Wall box: while not charging, press and hold A + C for 5 s  Settings 
           DLB Pairing  On  Confirm  Home. Blue DLB icon (flashing)
           indicates pairing mode.

      · Success: corresponding DLB link LED turns solid; wall box DLB icon
           becomes green. Press the Pairing Button once to finish pairing early.

   6.2 Operation

      · Full Current Charging (Green DLB icon and white current color):
           sufficient headroom; each wall box charges at its requested current.

      · Reduced Current Charging (Orange DLB icon and current color):
           headroom drops; DLB reduces and optimizes currents while keeping
           each branch 6 A where possible. Otherwise, that branch is paused.

      · Paused (Red DLB icon and current color): This wall box is paused due to
           severe current shortage. Recovery is automatic when headroom returns.

Appendix B -- 433 MHz Antenna Installation Recommendations

   · Antenna type: 433 MHz ¼ -wave whip with SMA and magnetic base.
   · Mounting: Install the DLB inside the distribution board for short Power

        and CT runs. Route the antenna cable out of the metal enclosure and
        mag-mount the base on the exterior metal surface (top or side).
   · Orientation: Keep the whip vertical. It's omnidirectional--no need to
        point it at the wall box.
   · Distance & path: Keep the DLB-wall box distance as short as practical
        and aim for a clear path with no large metal objects in between.
   · Height & placement: Prefer the top of the cabinet or another high, open
        spot; avoid corners, deep recesses, and spots shielded by beams/ducts.
   · Ground plane: A magnetic metal surface under the base acts as a ground
        plane--good for performance. Keep the whip a few centimeters away
        from other metal masses or rails.
   · Separation: Avoid coiling excess cable near power devices; keep several
        centimeters of clearance from large metal obstacles.
   · Cable routing: Stay away from high-current cables/VFDs; if you must
        cross them, do it at right angles. Provide basic strain relief; avoid sharp
        bends.
   · Connectors: Hand-tighten the SMA; check for a firm connection after
        positioning.
   · Environment: If mounted where moisture/dust is possible, add basic
        weather shielding (indoor use only unless otherwise rated).
   · Regulatory: Follow local ISM-band limits (transmit power/duty cycle).
